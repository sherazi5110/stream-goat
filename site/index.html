Stream Goat

Movies, series, anime and Live TV on Windows, Linux, Android and Android TV. Runs CloudStream extensions and Stremio add-ons. Free and open source.

Everything in one app

The same app and the same look on every device.

🧩

Extensions and add-ons

Add CloudStream repositories and Stremio add-ons by link, exactly as in the official apps.

🔎

Search suggestions

Titles drop down as you type; arrow keys and Enter pick one.

🌍

Translate subtitles

Into 24 languages, including Hindi, Urdu, Chinese, Arabic, Spanish and French, keeping every line's timing.

✨

Anime enhance

Low-resolution animation is enlarged with sharper filters (Lanczos on Windows and Linux, a Catmull-Rom GPU shader on Android), so line art stays crisp.

🌈

HDR

Real HDR10, HLG and Dolby Vision on HDR Android screens; colour correction for HDR on desktop.

📺

Live TV

M3U playlists with a TV guide, Xtream Codes logins and Stremio TV add-ons. YouTube, Twitch and Dailymotion channels play right in the app.

⬇️

Downloads

Like a download manager: 8 connections at once, the source and quality you choose, resume after pauses, with subtitles.

⏭️

Skip intro

Skip intros, recaps and credits in anime, and a countdown to the next episode.

👨‍👩‍👧

Profiles and kids mode

Everyone gets their own history and list; kids profiles with a parent PIN.

⭐

Ratings and artwork

TMDB, IMDb and AniList ratings, cast, trailers and backdrops.

▶️

VLC player built in

Plays almost any format, with source switching, online subtitles, audio languages and zoom.

🎨

Your look

Liquid glass, dark, AMOLED and light themes, any accent colour, and your own wallpaper. The goat logo in rainbow or your colour.

🐐

40 profile pictures

Goats in nine colours, a king, a DJ, a cowboy and more, plus 25 animals.

Screenshots

From the Windows and Linux app. Tap a picture to see it bigger.
