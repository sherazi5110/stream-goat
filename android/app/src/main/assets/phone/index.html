🐐 Send to Stream Goat

Add a Stremio add-on or a CloudStream repository to your TV without typing on the remote.

Stremio add-ons: the link ending in manifest.json (or a stremio:// link).
CloudStream repositories: the repository's link (…/repo.json), a cloudstreamrepo:// link, or its shortcode. Live TV playlists (.m3u) work too. Several at once: one per line.
