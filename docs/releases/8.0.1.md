## What's new in 8.0.1

- **Back up to Google Drive.** Sign in with your Google account (Settings → *Updates and backup* on Android,
  *Storage* on Windows/Linux): the app shows a code, you enter it at google.com/device on your phone, so it works on
  Android TV too. Your settings, profiles, My list, history, repositories, extensions and their settings, Live TV
  sources and Stremio add-ons are then backed up automatically whenever something changed. On a new device or after
  reinstalling, sign in and restore: the extensions download again. Stream Goat only sees its own hidden backup file
  in your Drive, never your other files.
- **Download like a download manager (IDM).** A file is fetched in 4 MB pieces over **8 connections at once**
  (Settings → Downloads/Storage → *Connections per download*: 1–16), and streams download several segments at a
  time. Finished pieces are kept, so a pause, a restart or a dropped connection loses nothing. The Downloads list
  shows the speed and the time left.
- **Choose what to download.** Pressing *Download* lists the sources: *Best (automatic)* or a specific source, and
  for a stream, its qualities (1080p, 720p, 480p…). *Download these* (a season) asks for one quality for all of
  them. Turn the question off with *Ask which source and quality*.
- **Android TV: add from your phone, no typing.** Extensions → **Add from phone** shows a QR code. Scan it with your
  phone (same Wi-Fi), paste a Stremio add-on link or a CloudStream repository (its link, a cloudstreamrepo:// link or
  a shortcode, so repositories without a shortcode work too) and tap *Send to TV*; then press **Add** on the TV.
  Several links at once, one per line; Live TV playlists work too. It only listens while that screen is open, only
  on your home network, and needs the 4-digit code shown on the TV.
- **Videos start sooner.** Android buffered 3 seconds of video before starting; now 1.5 s, like the Windows and
  Linux app. Found sources are kept for 20 minutes (as the official app does), so playing an episode again, or
  continuing it soon after, starts at once; if they've expired, the app looks again by itself. And when a title's
  page opens, its sources are already looked up in the background, so **Play** often starts straight away
  (Settings → Player → *Find sources before you press Play*).
- **More extensions work.** Checked every extension in the official app's repository list (34 repositories, 587
  extensions: Phisher, CSX, CNCVerse, MegaRepo, Aniyomi Compat, the Italian, German, Ukrainian, Arabic, Vietnamese,
  Indonesian, Brazilian, French and Desi repositories, 759 and more) against the apps without running them.
  - **Android: 577 of 587 link completely**, up from 571. Three more load and play with just their Chromecast
    buttons missing (M6+, RMC+, TF1+).
  - Added from the official app: extensions' own video actions, which M6+, RMC+, TF1+ and StreamCenter register
    while loading, so before they didn't load at all. Also the official text helpers (Aniyomi), saved
    bookmark/resume records (SyncProvider, Wrapped), installing other extensions and repositories (MegaRepo,
    AutoExtensions), the player's preferred audio language (PrimeVideo) and OpenSubtitles headers (3isk).
  - YouTube (official repository) works through NewPipe, and CineStream's Simkl account is supported.
  - **Windows and Linux** get the same classes, plus Android's XML parser types for 42 extensions' settings screens.
  - What's left also fails in the official app: StreamPlay's "atlanticWeb4K" server, PobreFlix and SkillShare
    (built for an older version). Some extensions change the official app's own screens or player, so they don't
    apply here: SleepGuard, WatchParty, most of StreamCenter, and Aniyomi's settings screen.
- **Android TV: faster, smoother start.** Like the official app, the extension your Home page uses loads first, so
  Home appears right away while the others load behind it. They used to load all at once on 64 threads, which could
  make the screen stutter on TV boxes; now as many load at once as the device has cores, below the screen's
  priority. Extension updates and the update check wait until the first screens have settled, and extensions that
  add repositories on every start (MegaRepo) no longer fetch them or refresh the screens each time.
- **Android: if the app closes, it says why.** The next start shows what went wrong and what was playing, to
  copy, share or photograph for a report. An extension failing in the background no longer closes the app: that
  extension stops and a message says so.
- **Continue Watching starts with the source you were using.** The source that last played a title is remembered,
  and Continue Watching or Resume starts it again as soon as the extension finds it, instead of waiting for every
  source. If it's gone, the best one plays as before.
- **Windows, Linux: the Z key opens the player's Picture menu** (zoom, Anime enhance, HDR colour fix), as its button
  and Settings said; **Shift+Z** cycles the zoom and **E** turns Anime enhance on or off. The Picture menu now says
  whether Anime enhance is sharpening the video you're watching (*"480p sharpened to 1080p"*) or has nothing to do.

Everything from 8.0.0 is included: the new goat look, YouTube/Twitch/Dailymotion channels in Live TV, channels that say
why they don't play, the Android TV fixes and more extensions on Windows and Linux.
